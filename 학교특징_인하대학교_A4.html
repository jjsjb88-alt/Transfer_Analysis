제목 단어 구역을 따로 두기보다 글의 연결을 계속 묻는다

반복 2022~2026 인문40문항은 독립 동의어·문법 구역보다 빈칸·제목·무관 문장·삽입·순서·내용을 지문 안에서 섞는다. 단독 지문과 두 문항 세트가 공존한다.

유지변화 2025에는5~8·26~29에 삽입을 모으고, 2026에는5~9가 삽입이다. 큰 읽기 방향은 이어지지만 발문 번호와 개수까지 고정되지는 않는다.

타교차이 중앙대의 앞 생소한 동의어·대화 구간과 대비된다. 인하는 관찰과 인과, 정의와 새 사례, 문장의 선행 대상을 바꾸어 확인한다. 자료의 보기 연결 오류를 출제 결함으로 오해하지 않아야 한다.

뜻: 두 현상 사이의 관계가 어떻게 생기는지는 아직 모른다.

함께 나타나는 관계는 관찰했지만 영향의 작동 원리는 앞으로 연구할 일입니다. 이미 영향을 입증했다고 바꾼 ②를 고릅니다.

정답 2 · The researchers found the evidence that the LRTC of alpha waves influences identity integration.

기출 본문 확인 · 원출처 미확정

Read the passage and answer the questions.

The researchers were looking at the long-range temporal correlation (LRTC) of alpha waves at the frontocentral lobe—an area of the brain associated with our concept of self and decision making. “For the past 20 years, cognitive neuroscience has been dominated by the approach of examining brain regions that are activated in response to external stimuli that trigger specific cognitive processes,” says Professor Nakao, who teaches psychology at Hiroshima University. “In other words, research has been conducted from the perspective of which part of the brain is responsible for a particular cognitive function.” [A] The research team noticed that if there was more random activity or so-called noise that interrupted the long memory of alpha waves at the frontocentral lobe, then participants in their study were more likely to experience identity confusion. [B] That confusion was measured as the subjective feeling of being temporally changeable and fragmented, indecisive, and being unable to commit to important life decisions. [C] Constructing a subjective identity involves the integration of different aspects of consciousness, since our sense of a unified self relies on multiple cognitive functions like our ability to process external and internal stimuli and inputs from our social environment, and to perceive the passing of time. [D] Psychologists and neuroscientists believe that the temporal dynamics of brain activity, like the LRTC of alpha waves, hold some valuable insights. They might help facilitate the cognitive processes necessary to form an integrated sense of self. [E] “Although the details of the mechanism behind the relationship between LRTC and identity are unknown and will be explored in the future, we can assume that LRTC provides temporal stability in the processing of external inputs, which contributes to the integration of identity,” says Nakao.

According to the passage, which of the following is not stated? [2점]

  1. Identity confusion may lead to inability to commit to important life decisions.
  2. The researchers found the evidence that the LRTC of alpha waves influences identity integration.
  3. The researchers found a relationship between interruption of the LRTC of alpha waves and identity confusion.
  4. The frontocentral lobe of the brain is associated with our concept of self and decision making.
  5. Neuroscientists have mainly focused on examining which part of the brain is responsible for a particular cognitive function.

기존 정답: 2

2022 20번②는 관찰된 관계를 이미 확인한 영향 기전으로 바꾼다. 글이 무엇을 관찰했고 무엇을 앞으로 연구할지를 구별하게 해 추론 범위를 점검할 수 있다.

전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

Read the passage and answer the questions.

The United States is a society composed of many groups of people, some of whom originally belonged to other societies. [A] Sociologists consider the United States a pluralistic society, meaning it is built of many groups. [B] They argue that, as societies modernize, these societies attract people from countries where there may be economic hardship, political unrest, or religious persecution. [C] Fleeing poverty and religious persecution, these immigrants arrived in waves from Europe and Asia and helped create the pluralism that makes the United States unique. [D] Some practices that are common in other societies will inevitably offend or contradict the values and beliefs of the new society. [E] Groups seeking to become part of a pluralistic society often have to give up many of their original traditions in order to fit in—a process known as assimilation.

Which is the example of the underlined assimilation? [2.4점]

  1. The society of the Yanomamo has fluid land boundaries. While outsiders would have a hard time determining where Yanomamo land begins and ends, the Yanomamo people have no trouble discerning which land is theirs and which is not.
  2. Although Islam was practiced in both parts of the country, the residents of East Pakistan spoke Bengali, while the residents of West Pakistan spoke Urdu. Geographic distance, language differences, and other factors proved insurmountable.
  3. The U.S. guarantees the equal rights to everyone. However, powerful informal mechanisms, such as prejudice and discrimination, work to keep many groups out of the political process or out of certain neighborhoods.
  4. Although Chinese immigrants started arriving in the U.S. 150 years ago, Chinese-American communities still follow some traditions, such as celebrating the Lunar New Year.
  5. When people arrive in the U.S. from other countries, they most likely speak a foreign language, but they learn at least some English. Their children are bilingual, and by the third generation, the language originally spoken is often lost.

기존 정답: 5

40번⑤의 세대별 언어 상실은 동화의 정의를 새 사례에 적용한다. 다만 이런 사례 선택을 맞힌 것만으로 문화 변동의 모든 양상을 분류할 수 있다고 보지는 않는다.
