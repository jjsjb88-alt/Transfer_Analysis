The Barnum effect is a common psychological phenomenon whereby individuals give high accuracy ratings to descriptions, called Barnum statements, of their personality that supposedly are tailored specifically to them, yet which are in fact vague and general enough to apply to a broad range of people. The techniques of creating the Barnum effect are used by fortune tellers, astrologers, and other practitioners to convince customers that they, the practitioners, are in fact endowed with a paranormal gift.
