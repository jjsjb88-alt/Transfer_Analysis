필자가 말하는 중심 생각은?
the fact that science keeps changing is a mark of its strength

뜻: 과학이 계속 바뀐다는 사실은 오히려 강점의 표시다.

필자는 수정할 수 있기 때문에 과학이 발전한다고 봅니다. 변하므로 못 믿겠다는 평가는 다른 사람의 입장이어서 ④와 구별합니다.

정답 4 · Changing theories of science are actually a sign of its strength.

확인된 출처: Science Keeps Changing. So Why Should We Trust It?
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

Read the following passages and answer the questions. [28~29]

As popular mistrust of expert opinion grows, we increasingly encounter the following skeptical argument about science: Historically, even well-established theories and findings have been overturned; therefore, science can't be trusted because it will eventually change again. The skeptics are right that science does not progress uniformly and steadily toward truth. Once, scientists believed in the four humors as the key to health, in phlogiston* as the essence of fire and in the ether as the carrier of light. Eggs were bad for you, then fine, then maybe bad again. Even Newtonian physics, once considered unshakable, was revised by Einstein. The history of science is indeed a graveyard of theories, but the fact that science keeps changing is a mark of its strength. It keeps changing because the world is complex and full of wonder. That isn't a problem; it's the engine that drives scientific progress. *phlogiston: 연소하는 물질에 포함된 가상의 물질

What is the main idea of the passage?

  1. Scientific progress has stopped in the modern era.
  2. Science cannot be trusted because it keeps changing.
  3. Old scientific theories were more reliable than modern ones.
  4. Changing theories of science are actually a sign of its strength.

기존 정답: 4

36번④는 과학의 수정 가능성을 강점으로 보는 필자 평가다. 이어37번은 같은 변화 사실을 불신의 근거로 삼는 회의론자를 묻는다. 한 사실과 서로 다른 평가 주체를 분리해 진단할 수 있다.

2026 · 영어 오전반 · 37번

같은 사실을 회의론자는 어떻게 보는가

회의론자가 과학을 불신하는 이유는?
even well-established theories and findings have been overturned

뜻: 널리 받아들인 이론과 발견조차 뒤집힌 적이 있다.

이번 질문은 필자가 아니라 회의론자의 이유를 묻습니다. 받아들인 이론도 수정되거나 버려진다는 ④를 해당 인물에게 붙여 읽습니다.

정답 4 · even widely accepted theories are later revised or abandoned

According to the passage, skeptics argue that science cannot be trusted because _________.

  1. scientists often disagree with one another
  2. many scientific findings are influenced by social trends
  3. scientific theories frequently contradict everyday experience
  4. even widely accepted theories are later revised or abandoned

기존 정답: 4

37번④는 첫 두 문장의 회의론을 직접 확인한다. 36번과 정답 번호가 같아도 근거의 주체는 다르므로, 정답만 맞춘 학생에게 관점 구별이 됐는지 한 번 더 설명하게 한다.
