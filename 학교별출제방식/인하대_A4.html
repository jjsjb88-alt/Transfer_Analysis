Deepfakes are often produced using generative adversarial networks (GANs), in which two different AI deep-learning models work together in a guessing game. Ⓐ One of the models creates the best possible replica of a real image or video and the other detects whether the replica is fake. If it detects an error from the replica, it reports on the differences between the replica and the original. Ⓑ In other words, the first algorithm produces a similar image and receives feedback on its creation from the second model and then adjusts the image making it appear more real. The process is repeated as many times as it takes until the second model does not detect any false imagery. Ⓒ Deepfakes may also be generated by diffusion models. In this method, a text prompt generates a visual representation of a concept, which is filled with noise, or random data values. Ⓓ Deepfakes are associated with vicious motives, including creating disinformation and generating confusion about politically important matters. Ⓔ The model then learns to create high-quality images by gradually removing the noise and generating an accurate visualization of the text prompt.
