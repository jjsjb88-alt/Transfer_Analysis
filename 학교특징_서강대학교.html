정답 3 · forgetting is as politically significant as remembering

기출 본문 확인 · 원출처 미확정
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

Read the passage and answer the questions.

(A) Jan Assmann’s theory of cultural memory was developed to explain how societies maintain continuity across centuries despite the inevitable disappearance of living witnesses. (B) He distinguishes between communicative memory, which consists of everyday recollections shared among contemporaries, and cultural memory, which is preserved through durable, symbolic forms such as texts, monuments, rituals, and canonical narratives. Communicative memory is limited in temporal reach, typically extending no further than three or four generations. (C) Cultural memory, by contrast, is intentionally stabilized. It relies on institutions and repeated acts of commemoration to maintain a connection to a distant past. These acts do not merely store information; they actively shape collective self-understanding by selecting, emphasizing, and ritualizing certain interpretations of history over others. Through this process, the past becomes a structured resource for the present rather than a neutral record of what once occurred. (D) Interestingly, Assmann’s early academic work focused on ancient Egyptian religion, particularly funerary texts and mortuary practices. This scholarly background later informed his broader reflections on memory, as ancient civilizations offered clear examples of how ritualized remembrance could sustain social order over millennia. (E) Crucially, Assmann argues that cultural memory is inseparable from power. Decisions about what is canonized, commemorated, or forgotten are bound up with struggles over authority and legitimacy. Memory, in this sense, functions not as an archive but as a normative framework that guides how societies interpret their origins and orient themselves toward the future.

It can be inferred from the passage that Assmann would most likely agree that ________.

  1. historical accuracy is the primary goal of collective memory
  2. all societies remember the past in the same way
  3. forgetting is as politically significant as remembering
  4. communicative memory is more reliable than cultural memory
  5. memory functions independently of social institutions

기존 정답: 3

문화적 기억의 선택·기념·망각을 권력과 연결해③을 검증합니다. 연결된25번은 학자 이력 소개와 주장의 직접 전개를 구분하므로 문장 내용과 논증에서의 기능을 함께 확인할 수 있습니다.
