기대와 이미 일어난 결과

본문과 가장 거리가 먼 설명은?
real income has stagnated since the late 1990s for a majority of Americans

뜻: 미국인 다수의 실질소득은 1990년대 말부터 정체돼 있다.

AI의 기술적 성과나 주가와 가계소득은 같은 지표가 아닙니다. 이미 소득이 크게 늘었다고 바꾼 ③이 본문과 다릅니다.

정답 3 · AI adoption has already triggered substantial gains in household income.

확인된 출처: Artificial Intelligence and the Modern Productivity Paradox: A Clash of Expectations and Statistics
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

아래 지문을 읽고 문제에 답하시오.

We live in an age of paradox. Systems using artificial intelligence match or surpass human level performance in more and more domains, leveraging rapid advances in other technologies and driving soaring stock prices. Yet measured productivity growth has declined by half over the past decade, and real income has stagnated since the late 1990s for a majority of Americans. We describe four potential explanations for this clash of expectations and statistics: false hopes, mismeasurement, redistribution, and implementation lags. While a case can be made for each, we argue that lags have likely been the biggest contributor to the paradox. The most impressive capabilities of AI, particularly those based on machine learning, have not yet diffused widely. More importantly, like other general purpose technologies, their full effects won't be realized until waves of complementary innovations are developed and implemented. The required adjustment costs, organizational changes, and new skills can be modeled as a kind of intangible capital. A portion of the value of this intangible capital is already reflected in the market value of firms. However, going forward, national statistics could fail to measure the full benefits of the new technologies and some may even have the wrong sign.

윗글의 내용과 가장 거리가 먼 것은?

  1. The development of complementary innovations and the delay in implementing AI help explain the apparent paradox.
  2. Non-physical capital such as organizational changes and innovative skills contributes to realizing AI's potential.
  3. AI adoption has already triggered substantial gains in household income.
  4. Quantitative analyses may misestimate the benefits generated by AI technologies.

기존 정답: 3

20번③은 AI의 기술적 성과·주가와 이미 실현된 가계소득 증가를 섞습니다. 기대되는 효과와 관측된 결과, 이행 지연이라는 설명을 나눠 읽게 하는 근거가 분명합니다.

자기편과 반대편의 이유

두 빈칸에 맞는 조합은?
He who knows only his own side of the case knows little of that.

뜻: 자기 쪽 이유만 아는 사람은 그 사안도 제대로 알지 못한다.

자기편만이 아니라 반대편 이유도 알아야 한다는 관계입니다. opposite side를 찾은 뒤 첫 빈칸 disproving도 문맥에 넣어 조합 ②를 확인합니다.

정답 2 · Ⓐ disproving / Ⓑ on the opposite side

확인된 출처: On Liberty
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

아래 지문을 읽고 문제에 답하시오.

The peculiar evil of silencing the expression of an opinion is, that it is robbing the human race; posterity as well as the existing generation; those who dissent from the opinion, still more than those who hold it. If the opinion is right, they are deprived of the opportunity of exchanging error for truth: if wrong, they lose, what is almost as great a benefit, the clearer perception and livelier impression of truth, produced by its collision with error. Complete liberty of contradicting and Ⓐ_________ our opinion is the very condition which justifies us in assuming its truth for purposes of action; and on no other terms can a being with human faculties have any rational assurance of being right. Strange it is that men should admit the validity of the arguments for free speech but object to their application. Unseasonable kindness to mankind may injure them more than severity. He who knows only his own side of the case knows little of that. His reasons may be good, and no one may have been able to refute them. But if he is equally unable to refute the reasons Ⓑ_________, if he does not so much as know what they are, he has no ground for preferring either opinion.

Ⓐ와 Ⓑ에 들어갈 가장 적절한 것을 고르시오.

  1. Ⓐ reprobating / Ⓑ on the same side
  2. Ⓐ disproving / Ⓑ on the opposite side
  3. Ⓐ conceding / Ⓑ on a speaker's side
  4. Ⓐ yielding / Ⓑ on both sides

기존 정답: 2

17번은 두 빈칸을 한 보기로 묶습니다. own side와 대조되는Ⓑ의 opposite side만 찾아도 답이 크게 좁혀져, 채점 결과만으로Ⓐ의 어휘 선택과Ⓑ의 관계 이해를 따로 진단하기는 어렵습니다.
