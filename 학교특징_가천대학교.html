정답 3 · The public school ethos helped sustain a professional elite opposed to commercial priorities.

본문 일치 판본: Education in Britain (공개 전재)
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

[23~40] Read the following passages and answer the questions.

The public schools in Britain were vital to the establishment of a particular set of values in the dominant professional middle classes. These values were reflected in the novel Tom Brown's Schooldays by Thomas Hughes, written in tribute to his own happy time at Rugby School. Its emphasis is on the making of gentlemen to enter one of the professions: law, medicine, the Church, the Civil Service or the colonial service. The concept of 'service', even if it only involved entering a profitable profession, was central to the public school ethos. A career in commerce was not to be considered. As a result of such values, the public school system was traditional in its view of learning and deeply resistant to science and technology. Most public schools were located in the 'timeless' countryside, away from the vulgarity of industrial cities.

Which of the following can be inferred from the passage?

  1. Public schools were deliberately designed to prevent students from entering industrial professions.
  2. The decline of science education in Britain can be mainly attributed to public schools.
  3. The public school ethos helped sustain a professional elite opposed to commercial priorities.
  4. Most middle-class families opposed industrialization on moral grounds.

기존 정답: 3

For Anthony Hopkins devotees, that percussive tone is precisely what marks him out — the hard, Ⓐ peremptory music of his speech. You hear it all over the place. He and Starling are conversing one to one, through the bars of a cage, but his diction is as crisp as a breadstick.

  1. desultory
  2. deferential
