[B] Also in these situations, it is generally the case that one linguistic group is in a more powerful position, economically or otherwise, such as the relationship of plantation owner to worker or enslaver to enslaved person. [C] For example, English is the superstrate language for Hawaiian Pidgin English, Swahili for the various forms of Pidgin Swahili spoken in East and Central Africa. [D] The other languages, substrate languages, also contribute to the lexicon and grammar, but in a less obvious way.
