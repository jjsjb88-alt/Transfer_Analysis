Because of this damage-deficit correlation, research on patients with aphasia has provided a great deal of information about how language is organized in the brain. Patients with injuries to Broca’s area may have Broca’s aphasia, as it is often called today. Broca’s aphasia is characterized by labored speech and certain kinds of word-finding difficulties, but it is primarily a disorder that affects a person’s ability to form sentences with the rules of syntax.
