기출 본문 1/1When the Maya empire fell to the Spanish in the 16th century, numerous documents written in the Maya script were destroyed. In addition, Mayans were forced to learn the Latin alphabet and to speak only in Spanish. As a result, the script that the Mayans had used prior to invasion eventually became impenetrable. The first to try to decode the Maya script were the conquistadors themselves. One of their number, Diego de Landa, described the script as an alphabet, having assumed that its symbols were analogous to the phonetic symbols of the Latin alphabet. By the 20th century, however, the Ⓐ __________ view was that the script was a pictographic system - that is, each of its symbols stood not for a single sound but for a whole word or idea. Then, in the 1950s, a Russian linguist named Yuri Knorozov chanced upon a replica of a Mayan text. Approaching the symbols with a fresh pair of eyes, Knorozov concluded that there were too few to form a pictographic system but too many to form an alphabet.
