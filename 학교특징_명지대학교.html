정답 4 · Dr. Fallon’s theory of psychiatric disorders has opened up a new horizon in criminal neuroanatomy.

기출 본문 확인 · 원출처 미확정
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.

James Fallon is a professor from UC Irvine in neuroanatomy. One of his major areas of study is psychiatric disorders and how those disorders are represented in the physical construction of the brain. He was studying the brain anatomy of murderers and schizophrenics, looking to understand how their brains differed from ours. For comparing and contrasting, he frequently scanned the brains of family and friends to use as a base. He would then compare them to the minds of psychos. One day, he was seated at his desk, relaxing in his office. He had stacks of brain scans in front of him. Bored, he noticed one scan on the table in front of him. Picking it up, he noted it showed particularly low activity in the region associated with empathy. Low activity in this region is common in serial killers and psychopaths. Intrigued, he did a deeper analysis of the scan and confirmed that according to his theory it was indeed the brain structure of a psychopath. But moments later he had a stark realization that brain was his own. Of course, Dr. Fallon thinks he is a normal person and also states he hasn’t killed anyone.

Which of the following is not stated or implied in the passage?

  1. Dr. Fallon’s hypothesis about criminals’ brain structure may be inaccurate.
  2. Dr. Fallon scanned brains of many people including criminals and ordinary people.
  3. Dr. Fallon thought that a low level of empathy is associated with criminal activities.
  4. Dr. Fallon’s theory of psychiatric disorders has opened up a new horizon in criminal neuroanatomy.

기존 정답: 4

17번④의 학문적 새 지평이라는 성과 평가는 본문에 없습니다. 자기 뇌 스캔을 보고 이론을 되돌아보는 사건과 연구의 업적 평가를 구분하게 하는 점이 분명합니다.

정답 1 · Barnum statements contain personality descriptions applicable to nearly anyone.

본문 일치 판본: Barnum effect
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.

The Barnum effect is a common psychological phenomenon whereby individuals give high accuracy ratings to descriptions, called Barnum statements, of their personality that supposedly are tailored specifically to them, yet which are in fact vague and general enough to apply to a broad range of people. This effect can provide a partial explanation for the widespread acceptance of some paranormal beliefs and practices. Psychologist Paul E. Meehl coined the term “Barnum effect” because he relates the vague personality descriptions used in certain “pseudo-successful” psychological tests to those given by showman P. T. Barnum. The techniques of creating the Barnum effect are used by fortune tellers, astrologers, and other practitioners to convince customers that they, the practitioners, are in fact endowed with a paranormal gift.

Which of the following is true of the passage?

  1. Barnum statements contain personality descriptions applicable to nearly anyone.
  2. The Barnum effect reveals that all individuals have their own unique personalities.
  3. P.T. Barnum was the first to report and propose the effect from his experiments.
  4. P.T. Barnum was endowed with a paranormal gift of penetrating into the human mind.

기존 정답: 1

22번①은 바넘 진술의 첫 정의를 거의 직접 환언합니다. 개념의 확인에는 명료하지만, 새 성격 진술이 왜 바넘 효과를 만드는지 적용하는 문항은 아니어서 정의와 사례 적용을 따로 점검할 필요가 있습니다.
