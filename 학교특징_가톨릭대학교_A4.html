정답 2 · Banana plants grown from conventional suckers mature faster than tissue-cultured plants.

확인된 출처: Pocket K No. 14: Tissue Culture Technology
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

[34~36] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.

Tissue culture” is the cultivation of plant cells, tissues, or organs on specially formulated nutrient media. Under the right conditions, an entire plant can be regenerated from a single cell. This technology is considered particularly important for developing countries, as it facilitates the production of disease-free, high-quality planting material and enables the rapid production of large numbers of uniform plants. Kenya, for example, has greatly benefited from tissue culture technology. In Kenya, as in many tropical and subtropical developing regions, bananas are a vital food crop. __________, over the last two decades, banana production has experienced a significant decline due to widespread soil degradation, pest infestations, and diseases in banana orchards. These issues were exacerbated by the common practice of propagating new banana plants using infected suckers (young offshoots from the parent plant). Ⓐ This situation posed a serious threat to food security, employment, and income levels in banana-producing areas. Ⓑ With proper management and field hygiene, yield losses caused by pests and diseases have been significantly reduced at the farm level. Ⓒ Farmers now benefit from large quantities of superior, clean planting materials that mature earlier, taking only 12 to 16 months compared to the conventional banana, which requires two to three years. Ⓓ Additionally, tissue-cultured bananas produce bigger bunch weights, ranging from 30 to 45 kilograms, as opposed to the 10 to 15 kilograms yielded by conventional plants. Annual yields per unit of land have also improved substantially, with farmers achieving 40 to 60 tons per hectare compared to the previous 15 to 20 tons. In light of these developments, tissue culture technology can be seen as a transformative tool, one that helps address critical agricultural challenges, improving food security and productivity.

Which of the following is NOT true according to the passage?

  1. Kenya’s banana production declined due to soil deterioration, pest problems, and diseases.
  2. Banana plants grown from conventional suckers mature faster than tissue-cultured plants.
  3. Tissue culture bananas generally produce heavier bunches than those grown traditionally.
  4. Tissue culture technology has increased total yield per hectare in banana farming.

기존 정답: 2

34~36번은 같은 조직배양 지문에서 대조의 연결어, 해결책의 위치, 재배기간의 비교를 나눠 묻습니다. 36번②의 비교 방향까지 확인하므로 학생이 관계·구조·사실 중 어디서 막혔는지 구분하기 좋습니다.

정답 2 · Print dictionaries once enjoyed a level of commercial success surpassed only by the Bible.

확인된 출처: Is the Dictionary Done For?
전체 글·질문·보기·상세 해설

[21~28] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.

Once, every middle-class home contained a piano and a dictionary. The piano existed so families could hear music before phonographs were affordable and, later, so adults could torture children by insisting they master an instrument few ever play well. The dictionary’s role was more practical: settling domestic arguments over the spelling of “camaraderie,” the meaning of “sesquipedalian,” or the pronunciation of “puttee.” Occasionally, it helped with homework or a particularly cutthroat game of Scrabble. This was the world not very long ago. As late as the nineteen-eighties, Merriam-Webster’s Collegiate Dictionary spent 155 consecutive weeks on the New York Times best-seller list, selling fifty-seven million copies — second only, it was believed, to the Bible. There was real money in words. Then came the internet, offering instant answers to every lexical question imaginable. Today, it is nearly impossible to misspell a word on a computer, and even commas are difficult to misplace — though students still manage it. Like the rest of the analog world, dictionaries have had to adapt or quietly fade away.

According to the passage, which of the following is true?

  1. Pianos were mainly kept in middle-class homes for professional music training.
  2. Print dictionaries once enjoyed a level of commercial success surpassed only by the Bible.
  3. This household culture of pianos and dictionaries disappeared before the 1970s.
  4. Digital spell-checking has eliminated punctuation errors in student writing altogether.

기존 정답: 2

사전 지문의21번②는 성경 다음의 판매량이라는 국소 근거와 직접 대응합니다. 이 정답만으로 종이 사전의 문화적 역할과 디지털 전환이라는 글 전체의 관계를 읽었는지는 확인하기 어렵습니다.

서울시립대와 어떤 점이 닮았나

읽기와 빈칸을 함께 준비한다는 점이 닮았습니다. 최근 서울시립대는 두 문항 세트의 비중이 크고, 가톨릭대에는 연결·위치·사실을 세 갈래로 묻는 묶음도 있습니다.
